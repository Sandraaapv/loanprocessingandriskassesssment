UNDERWRITING LIFECYCLE

Three-stage automated underwriting framework

Designed to assist credit officers and underwriting committees with mathematical certainty rather than black-box guesses.

STAGE 01

Financial Intake & Normalization

Ingests applicant financials, debt obligations, coapplicant assets, and credit bureau scores with automated DTI calculation.

STAGE 02

Random Forest Pipeline

Evaluates 11 non-linear risk dimensions against 500 decision trees to produce calibrated approval probability and risk scores.

STAGE 03

Explainable Attribution

Extracts positive and negative credit factors, providing transparent, audit-ready justification for each decision.
