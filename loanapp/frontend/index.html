Frequently Asked Questions

What is FOIR and why is it capped at 45%?
Fixed Obligation to Income Ratio (FOIR) measures total monthly debt obligations against gross income. A 45% cap guarantees a safe buffer for household expenses and emergencies.
Are there pre-payment penalties on home loans?
Under Reserve Bank of India (RBI) regulations, floating-rate home loans to individual borrowers carry zero pre-payment penalties.
How does co-applicant income enhance headroom?
Adding a co-applicant expands household monthly income (e.g. ₹65,000 + ₹35,000 = ₹1,00,000), unlocking higher allowable borrowing headroom.
